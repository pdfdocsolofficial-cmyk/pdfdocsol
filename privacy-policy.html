This policy explains what information PDFDocSol (pdfdocsol.com, "we", "our") collects and how it is used when you visit the website.

Your files

Our PDF and image tools run inside your web browser. The files you select are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers, so we cannot see, store or share them.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of the visit. We use this data to keep the site secure, fix errors and understand how the site is used. We may also use analytics tools that collect anonymous usage statistics.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies allows it and its partners to show ads to you based on your browsing on our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's partner sites page.

Visitors from the EU, UK and other regions

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, California, Canada, Australia or New Zealand, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Where required, we ask for your consent before ads cookies are used. Contact us to make a request.

Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
