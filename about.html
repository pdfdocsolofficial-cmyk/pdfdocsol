About PDFDocSol

PDFDocSol is a set of free online tools that make everyday PDF and image jobs quick and simple. Merge files, split pages, compress documents, add page numbers, convert images and more, without installing software or creating an account.

Private by design

Every tool runs inside your browser. Your documents are not uploaded to a server, so contracts, invoices and personal papers stay on your device.

What you can do

Merge, split, rotate, crop, resize, organize and compress PDFs. Add page numbers, text, watermarks and signatures. Convert PDF pages to JPG, PNG or text, turn images and text files into PDF, and compress, resize or convert images.

Free and supported by ads

The tools are free to use. We show a small number of ads to cover the cost of running the website.
